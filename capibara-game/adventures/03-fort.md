# The Ghost of the Abandoned Fort

Story and puzzle treatment. All character and place names remain undecided.

## Story

A harbor resident brings news of a ghost at the old fort: its bell rings after sunset, pale footprints cross the beach, and a hollow voice calls from the walls. The harbor's most boastful pirate agrees to help only if someone finds his lucky slippers. The shrimp thinks this is a sensible precaution and asks whether slippers come in sets of ten.

The capybara treats the story as a mystery worth understanding. At the fort's outer gate, the duo discovers that the bell rings whenever the wind turns an old ventilation vane. A listening game reveals that the hollow voice comes through speaking tubes once used to carry messages around the building.

Inside, they find an anxious young seal preparing a surprise shadow-puppet show for a relative's birthday. The seal borrowed the empty fort for rehearsals. A pot of washable glowing stage paint spilled on the floor, leaving luminous tracks. Its requests for help traveled through the tubes and sounded like ghostly voices.

The rehearsal screen has collapsed, and the seal cannot reach the bell rope to announce the show. The duo offers to help. The boastful pirate, now wearing his slippers, admits that he was afraid and volunteers to invite the audience. The shrimp proudly joins the cast as a sea monster, although his silhouette requires considerable enlargement.

The finale reuses the mysterious sounds and lights as parts of the performance. Children see exactly how the supposed haunting was produced, and the fort becomes a welcoming place for future shows.

## Locations and progression

Harbor costume stall → fort gate and beach → courtyard → speaking-tube passage → rehearsal room. Solving the slippers task recruits a helper; inspecting the fort's clues uncovers the performer.

## Puzzle chain

| Step | Goal and clue | Player action | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Find the slippers | A picture on the pirate's cabin wall shows his distinctive pair. A delivery tag says they went to the costume stall for repairs. | Find the matching sole pattern among three repaired pairs at the stall and return them. | The pirate joins the trip and can hold the heavy fort gate open later. |
| 2. Open the gate | A counterweight bucket hangs above the gate; its chain has slipped out of the guide. | Let the shrimp inspect a small service hatch and re-seat the chain. Ask the pirate to raise the counterweight while the captain secures the gate's existing support catch. | The courtyard becomes accessible and remains open. |
| 3. Investigate the bell | The bell rings as a loose cord catches the wind vane. Its movement can be observed as often as needed. | Use the nearby boat hook to lift the cord free. Test the vane again. | Explain the ringing and keep the cord for the final announcement mechanism. |
| 4. Trace the voice | Pressing each speaking-tube funnel produces a different sound. A tapping reply comes from one branch. | Match a short, replayable tapping pattern, then follow its picture symbol to the corresponding passage. | Reach the rehearsal room and meet the seal. No rhythm timing is required. |
| 5. Reproduce the footprints | The seal shows a drawing of the intended luminous stage markings and admits spilling the paint. | Mix a fresh batch using reusable glow base and the requested color; stamp a sample beside the footprints. | Confirm the harmless source of the tracks and prepare markings for the show. |
| 6. Rebuild the stage | A diagram shows two support poles, a crossbar, and a screen. One peg has rolled beneath a platform. | The captain holds the frame; the shrimp retrieves and fits the small peg. Hang the folded screen from the costume box and mark safe standing spots. | The stage is ready. |
| 7. Announce the show | The seal can reach a low handle but not the high bell rope. | Route the recovered cord through an existing low guide and attach it to the bell rope. | The seal rings the bell; the pirate brings in the audience for a short performance. |

## Replay variations

- Slipper sole symbols vary with the cabin picture and matching repaired pair.
- The replying speaking tube and tapping sequence change together. Every pattern can be replayed and answered by selecting pictures.
- The stage-paint recipe requests one of two colors. The footprint tint, recipe drawing, and seal's explanation match that version.

## Hints and completion rules

The fort is gently mysterious, with warm pools of light and reassuring dialogue. The player can always return to the harbor. The show is announced as an eventual goal, never a countdown.

The pirate waits at the gate after being recruited and cannot leave the player trapped. The gate catch locks open permanently. No climbing or fast reactions are required. Incorrect sound choices produce a friendly reply; paint can be remixed indefinitely. The costume stall stays open and all construction pieces have fixed, accessible sources. The final show begins only when the player chooses to invite the audience.
