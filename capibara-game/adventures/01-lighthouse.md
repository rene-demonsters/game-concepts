# The Lighthouse That Went Dark

Story and puzzle treatment. All character and place names remain undecided.

## Story

The harbor is preparing for its floating lantern festival. The capybara captain is hanging a lantern on her ship when the lighthouse suddenly stops turning. Across the bay, a light flashes from an old wreck. The shrimp immediately reports an unauthorized ghost ship.

The lighthouse keeper is on the harbor side of the bay, repairing the festival foghorn. Before the crew can sail over, they help finish it and learn the safe approach through the shoals. At the lighthouse, the front door is jammed. The shrimp squeezes beneath it and discovers that a fallen broom is blocking the way. Working together, the pair gets inside.

Upstairs, the turning mechanism is tangled in festival bunting. A young octopus has taken shelter behind its wheel and is holding tightly to the loose end. The captain leaves the mechanism switched off while they work out how to persuade their visitor into the open.

From a window, the shrimp recognizes the wreck's mysterious flashing as a request for a landing signal. A salvage worker is polishing an old reflector there; its glints created the apparent ghost ship. Once the duo sends the right signal, the worker brings over a basket of recovered lighthouse equipment, including a little inspection lamp.

The lamp helps the crew look behind the wheel. They discover that the octopus has lost its shell-shaped comfort toy in the tangle. Returning the toy and offering a quiet bucket den gives it somewhere inviting to settle. The captain untangles the bunting, tests the wheel, and restarts the lighthouse.

In the finale, the rescued reflector throws warm light over the festival. The octopus watches from its bucket beside the keeper. The shrimp announces that the ghost ship has been successfully promoted to an ordinary boat.

## Locations and progression

Harbor workshop → buoy channel → lighthouse landing → lighthouse interior and lamp room. The wreck is visible from the shore; the salvage worker travels to the landing after receiving the signal.

## Puzzle chain

| Step | Goal and clue | Player action | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Repair the foghorn | Pressing its bellows produces a wheeze. A loose hose visibly flaps beside the horn. | Retrieve the hose clamp from an open workshop tray, attach the hose, and close the clamp. | The keeper demonstrates the horn and shares a picture chart of the safe buoy route. |
| 2. Find the channel | The chart shows three buoy symbols in order. Matching symbols appear in the bay. | Select the matching route on the illustrated travel map. | Reach the lighthouse landing. Incorrect choices leave the boat safely at the harbor with a spoken clue. |
| 3. Open the door together | The door moves slightly, then stops. The shrimp can inspect the drainage opening. | Send him beneath the door. Take a thin wooden batten from the landing's repair rack; use it beneath the door while he guides the captain. | Push aside the broom and open the lighthouse. |
| 4. Identify the flashing light | An illustrated signal card beside the window matches the wreck's flashes. | Compare the repeating flash pattern with the card. | Learn which colored landing signal the salvage worker needs. |
| 5. Make the signal screen | A craft station has reusable red, yellow, and blue dyes, a rinse basin, and a blank cloth screen. Picture recipes show orange and green. | Mix the requested color, dye the screen, and place it over the landing's hand lamp. | The salvage worker docks and lends the inspection lamp. Wrong mixtures can be rinsed out indefinitely. |
| 6. Retrieve the comfort toy | With the drive switched off, the lamp reveals a toy beneath the wheel. A service slot is too small for the captain. | Light the slot, then let the shrimp crawl through and pass out the toy. | The octopus becomes interested in leaving its hiding place. |
| 7. Restart the lighthouse | The octopus reaches toward its toy but wants a sheltered place to sit. An empty bucket and folded towel are beside the keeper's chair. | Make a bucket den, place the toy inside, and wait for the octopus to climb in. Remove the bunting and turn the test crank. | The keeper confirms the mechanism is clear; the captain switches the drive on. |

## Replay variations

- The landing signal requests orange or green, with matching recipe, flash card, dialogue, and finished screen.
- The clamp can be in one of two open workshop trays. Both are available before departure.
- The comfort toy rests behind either of two service slots. The inspection lamp reveals its actual position before the shrimp enters.

## Hints and completion rules

The captain reminds the player of the current problem; the shrimp points out a useful visible detail; a final hint identifies the interaction. For the signal puzzle, the progression is “We need the color on the card,” then “The recipe pictures show two dyes,” then the exact pair.

The workshop remains accessible after sailing. Dye, rinsing, and signal attempts are unlimited. The inspection lamp is delivered without requiring entry into the blocked mechanism. The octopus never damages or consumes an item. The wheel cannot run while either character is inspecting it. Completing the signal puzzle ends the wreck's flashing so the explanation is visible in the world.
