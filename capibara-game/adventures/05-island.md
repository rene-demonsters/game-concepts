# The Island That Disappeared

Story and puzzle treatment. All character and place names remain undecided.

## Story

A baker asks the crew to deliver a birthday cake to a small island. The old chart shows a low shore beside a tall, forked rock, but when the capybara and shrimp arrive, all they see is water and a wall of cliffs. The shrimp checks beneath the chart in case the island has slipped out.

Back at the harbor, three residents offer apparently contradictory directions. A fisher remembers a sandy path. A post worker remembers a stone arch. A visitor remembers approaching a sheltered lagoon beneath a high cliff. The crew collects their picture messages and compares them with the chart.

Their investigation reveals that the old chart shows the area at low tide. The island sits inside a lagoon behind the headland; its low outer sandbank is covered at high tide. A rock arch forms the familiar short approach, but the passage has enough clearance only when the water is low. The island has never moved, and its residents are happily preparing the birthday party, unaware that the visitor cannot find the entrance.

The harbor tide board is missing a sliding marker. The shrimp retrieves it from behind the board while the captain holds the panel steady. Using the repaired board and their collected clues, they plan a low-tide visit. Choosing to prepare at the harbor advances the story to the correct tide, with no actual waiting.

At the exposed landing, a small cart's wheel has slipped into a drainage gap. The duo repairs it, loads the cake, and follows the sandy approach into the lagoon. The party host welcomes them with a freshly drawn chart that includes both the outer headland and the hidden island.

The shrimp is pleased that the island has been recovered. The capybara gently points out that it was their directions that needed finding. They share cake while he inspects the new chart for any other missing land.

## Locations and progression

Harbor bakery and tide board → cliff lookout → fisher's landing, post station, and visitor's jetty → low-tide arch landing → hidden lagoon and party garden. The three clue visits can happen in any order.

## Replay variations

- The destination is behind one of two headlands, with matching chart, telescope silhouette, messages, and travel-map marker.
- Letter emblems and sketch-board layouts vary within small authored sets.
- The cart's loose cap lies in either of two visible drainage branches, both reachable by the shrimp.

## Hints and completion rules

The tide explanation uses side-by-side pictures and spoken descriptions. Children do not need to read a clock or understand a tide timetable. The captain can recap the collected clues at the route board at any time.

The cake has no freshness meter or deadline. The three route-clue favors cannot consume one another's items. Travel remains available between every visited location. Once low tide is selected, it stays low until the delivery is complete; no route closes while the duo explores. Both headland variants have the same accessible supporting locations. The original delivery strap remains attached throughout, so repairing the cart cannot accidentally remove the cake's protection.
