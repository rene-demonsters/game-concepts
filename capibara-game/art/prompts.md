# Concept art generation prompts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. These independent illustrations use the same scene and character brief to compare art directions. All names remain undecided.

The fourth, animation-conscious direction has a separate [flat cutout prompt](04-flat-cutout-prompt.md).

The comic direction adapts [the repository's comic-book guide](../../art-styles/comic-book.md) to a peaceful children's adventure. The third direction is a gouache and layered-paper storybook treatment.

## 01-comic-book.png

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: landscape game concept illustration for an original children's point-and-click pirate adventure.
Scene: A welcoming island lighthouse landing at blue-hour dusk. A short wooden jetty leads to a squat round lighthouse with its lamp dark; a small friendly sailing boat is moored beside it. A few warm festival lanterns hang on ropes. Across the calm water a distant old shipwreck reflects a small mysterious glint. The mystery feels intriguing and completely safe.
Foreground subjects: a female anthropomorphic capybara child captain, sturdy rounded body, unmistakable broad blunt rectangular capybara muzzle, tiny rounded ears high on her head, brown fur, short sturdy legs, no visible tail. She wears a simple teal short captain's jacket, ochre neckerchief, and a rust-red headscarf, no weapons. Calm, attentive, friendly expression. Beside her on a raised wooden step stands her much smaller shrimp first mate, about one fifth her height, coral pink segmented curved body, expressive black eyes on short stalks, long delicate antennae, little walking legs, tiny navy vest. The shrimp is an actual recognizable shrimp, no large crab claws, no lobster pincers. He excitedly points toward a loose lighthouse door with a narrow drain opening below it. She holds a slim wooden batten and considers his discovery. Both are readable and important in the composition; the shrimp has an uncluttered contrasting background.
Composition: single cohesive wide 3:2 scene, three-quarter side view at character height, like a polished playable adventure-game location. Characters in foreground, lighthouse entrance and drainage opening in middle ground, sea and wreck in background. Important objects clearly separated, restrained environmental clutter. No inventory UI.
Mood: warm friendship, gentle mystery, exploratory humor, suitable for ages 4–8.
Constraints: no character or location names, no words, lettering, speech balloons, captions, logos, watermark, panel borders or montage. No violence, fighting, weapons, skulls, gore, menace or distressed characters. Do not imitate any existing entertainment character.
Style/medium: expressive comic-book illustration with confident bold ink contours, solid deep-plum shadow shapes, crisp cel shading, strong silhouettes and expressive faces. Tactile paper grain, screenprint texture and restrained halftone within selected shadows. Cream and warm candy pink, saturated turquoise accents, deep plum; warm lantern light against graphic evening shadows. Calm deliberate staging with playful expressions. A polished standalone illustration, not a comic page. Avoid photorealism, smooth 3D rendering, watercolor washes and muddy blending.
```
