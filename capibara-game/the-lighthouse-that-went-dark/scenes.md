# Scenes and hubs

IDs are stable references for puzzles and asset work. Left/right directions describe the player's view. Each scene needs a clean background, separate interactive props, explicit exit targets, character standing zones, and room for the inventory along the bottom. Clues must remain visible at normal game size. No essential object should be a tiny pixel hunt.

## S01 — Home jetty: opening and return

**Composition:** the duo's snug ship sits left, a broad jetty fills the lower middle, the lighthouse rises across the water on the right. A suspended festival lantern frames the sky. Reserve the jetty center for the heroes and the right edge for the square exit.

**Purpose:** establish the duo, show the lighthouse failure, and introduce the calm festival delay. The ship opens the travel map after the route conversation. Clicking the distant wreck replays a brief flashing loop and shrimp speculation.

**Hotspots:** hanging lantern, ship/map, lighthouse silhouette, water, square exit. No required inventory item originates here. A fish jumps through the shrimp's reflection as an optional joke.

**States:** beam turning at opening → beam dark through investigation → beam restored and lantern flotilla at ending. Keep the sky at the same storybook dusk until the ending; there is no clock.

**Characters:** captain, shrimp, pelican coordinator at opening and finale. Ending adds keeper, seal, and octopus with den.

## S02 — Harbor square: harbor hub

**Composition:** an open semicircular square around a dry decorative anchor. Jetty exit lower left, repair shed upper left, craft quay right. A low market awning supports a gull nest and the missing picture chart. The nest is reachable by a friendly gull conversation; the captain never climbs.

**Purpose:** orient the player and introduce requests. Three exits stay visually distinct. The coordinator repeats the overall goal and can point to the keeper. The gull wants soft nest lining before releasing the chart (P01).

**Hotspots:** gull/nest/chart, coordinator, exits, anchor, decorations. Chart remains an obvious oversized paper silhouette. A hidden clamp may occupy an open toolbox by the anchor (P02 placement variant). No item is inside an unmarked decorative container.

**States:** chart in nest / exchanged; optional clamp present / removed; festival waiting / underway. The gull visibly places the cloth in its nest after the exchange. Clicking the anchor makes a family of snails salute.

## S03 — Repair shed: listening and fixing

**Composition:** open-front shed, foghorn and flapping hose center, low tool tray left, spare-part rack right, clear doorway toward square. The turtle keeper stands next to the horn, without covering the hose.

**Purpose:** diagnose and repair the foghorn (P02). The keeper explains the chart and navigation; she only boards after chart and repair are complete. A fat hose clamp can be in the tool tray. A cloth offcut can hang from the rack (P01 variant).

**Hotspots:** horn test, hose socket, tray, rack, keeper, exit. Clicking the horn before repair makes a friendly wheeze and visibly lifts the loose hose. Applying the clamp includes seating the hose automatically; no separate invisible action.

**States:** loose hose / clamped hose, clamp and cloth availability, keeper present / aboard / returned for finale. With keeper aboard, a picture note and replayable remembered dialogue preserve guidance. Optional hanging spoon chimes play a short tune.

## S04 — Craft quay: supplies and colors

**Composition:** sheltered dye stall left, rinse basin below it, broad drying rack right, harbor water beyond. A sea-otter craft host stays behind the table. A spare screen and reusable dye dispensers have large shaped labels.

**Purpose:** find the cloth offcut or sail patch and solve P06 after seeing the balcony card. Crafting is available early, but the host explains that the destination card tells which color to send. Arbitrary experimentation cannot spend materials.

**Hotspots:** offcut basket, drying rack/patch, dye station, recipes, rinse basin, square exit. Offcut and sail patch have different silhouettes. Recipe pictures pair orange with a sun symbol and green with a leaf; the same symbols appear at the balcony.

**States:** offcut/patch present or collected; screen undyed, orange, green, or muddy. Dyes and mixed screen are generated at the station; the filled screen is one inventory item. Rinsing returns an undyed screen. Optional paper fish on the rack dances when clicked.

## S05 — Buoy channel: one navigation screen

**Composition:** ship at the bottom, three broad successive rows of buoy choices receding into the bay, lighthouse destination beyond. Match chart pictograms to buoy shapes; symbols remain readable without color.

**Purpose:** P03. The player chooses one buoy at a time for a three-symbol route. Display and replay the chart at any point. A wrong choice resets the current row while the boat bobs safely in place. Nothing strikes a reef.

**States:** route not solved / first, second, third choice / solved. After success, travel is automatic on future journeys, with an optional replay button. This screen has no collectible item or locked side route. Click a floating cork for a polite crab wave.

## S06 — Lighthouse landing: island hub

**Composition:** lighthouse door upper left; broad stone landing center; low repair rack and bucket right; cove path far right; returning ship/map lower left; signal lamp on a low pedestal beside the sea. The tiny drain sits beside the door at a usable touch-target size.

**Purpose:** P04 blocks the interior, while cove and return travel remain open. The shrimp discovers the broom through the drain. The boatbuilder's path and the bucket can be explored before entry. The keeper waits here until the door opens.

**Hotspots:** door, drain, bucket, rack (possible sail patch), cove path, ship, signal pedestal. The pedestal accepts the colored screen only once the balcony card is understood; early clicks explain how to find the instruction.

**States:** door ajar / open; bucket present / removed; patch present / removed; lamp unscreened / orange / green; worker absent / moored. The S11 vignette uses a separate boat overlay on the same background. A line of limpets echoes the shrimp's salute.

## S07 — Boatbuilder cove: trade with a reason

**Composition:** small sailboat on safe wooden supports left, crab boatbuilder at a low workbench center, batten rack right, landing path in foreground. No boat blocks the exit.

**Purpose:** P04 prerequisite. The crab has a spare thin batten, but needs his distinctive crescent sail patch before returning to work. He lent it to the festival craft table; the host may have sent it to the landing rack. Dialogue matches the actual placement. Returning it rewards the batten immediately; applying the patch is automatic.

**Hotspots:** crab/request, torn sail outline, batten rack, exit. Clicking the batten first reveals the exchange naturally. The player can collect the patch before hearing the request. Optional carved wooden ducks quack in descending sizes.

**States:** sail torn / patched; batten reserved / lent. No additional inventory reward or unexplained barter chain.

## S08 — Keeper's nook: comfort clues

**Composition:** a warm round room with stairs to machine room right, outside door left, low chair and towel rail center, empty laundry basket beside chair, octopus picture on a shelf. An oval rug provides a clear assembly area for the den.

**Purpose:** supply the towel (P08) and explain the visitor's wish for cover. The keeper recalls a little octopus clutching a shell toy. The empty basket has sucker prints, echoing those upstairs. The toy itself is never placed here.

**Hotspots:** towel, basket, keeper, stairs, exit, rug. Combine bucket and towel in inventory or at the rug; both routes make the same den item. The keeper can repeat a description of the toy.

**States:** towel present / removed; keeper dialogue before / after octopus identification. Optional teapot whistles a two-note reply to the shrimp.

## S09 — Machine room: cooperative rescue

**Composition:** large stopped wheel left-center, visible bunting caught in it, dark recess behind wheel, two low service slots, octopus eyes and tentacle peeking from cover. Clear floor area right holds the den target. Nook stairs and balcony stairs are both visible.

**Purpose:** diagnose P05, retrieve toy in P07, settle visitor in P08, and restart in P09. On entry, the keeper explicitly disconnects the drive. Neither service slot requires reaching past a moving part.

**Hotspots:** keeper/disconnect, wheel, bunting, left/right service slot, octopus, den target, test crank, switch, both exits. Lamp use illuminates the selected slot; the toy silhouette is visible only in its configured slot. Clicking the revealed toy triggers both heroes' cooperative retrieval.

**States:** drive disconnected → toy revealed/retrieved → den placed/toy placed → octopus settled → fabric clear → tested → running. Never restart with a hero or octopus in the mechanism. Incorrect targets get a calm clue.

**Optional joke:** a motionless toy weather vane beside the window spins when the shrimp blows at it; it is visually separate from the actual mechanism.

## S10 — Signal balcony: translate a clue

**Composition:** enclosed, waist-high railing in foreground, wreck visible across the water left, large pictorial signal card right, fixed telescope center. Heroes stand well back from the railing.

**Purpose:** P05 identifies the flashing source and records P06's requested color/symbol. A telescope close-up shows a seal shape, cloth movement, and reflective surface; no ghost. The card provides the exact flash-to-color association, so no Morse-code knowledge is needed.

**Hotspots:** telescope, card, wreck, stairs down. Card and close-up carry the current variant. Hearing/seeing the full flash loop records it automatically, with replay and a persistent picture reminder in the map. A child may click the matching card row; errors are harmless and repeat the demonstration.

**States:** repeated flashing / worker rowing / quiet wreck after arrival; active sun or leaf request on card. Optional telescope click spots a gull wearing a seaweed mustache.

## S11 — Wreck explanation: landing vignette

No new background. The seal rows into S06 after the correct signal, with reflector and recovered equipment basket. Dialogue explains both the flashes and the waiting rule. The lamp is placed in a broad takeable spot, then automatically offered if the player leaves without it. The worker stays available to lend it. The reflector remains a story prop, never a required inventory item.

## S12 — Festival: player-triggered finale

Reuse S01/S02 with lit lantern, flotilla, restored beam, and party cast overlays. The coordinator offers a large picture “Ready” action only after P09. Before that, all locations remain accessible. After the finale, allow a safe celebration view and credits replay; starting another adventure requires an explicit new-game action.
