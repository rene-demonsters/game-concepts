# Comic book / action comic

A reusable art direction based on the action-comic concept art explored for *Make Yourself at Home*. Keeping this reference does not select it as that game's final style.

## The look

Expressive comic illustration with bold ink contours, solid black or deep plum shadow shapes, and crisp cel shading. Characters have clear silhouettes, exaggerated poses, and readable expressions. Environments remain detailed enough to tell a story without competing with the main action.

The finish resembles a printed illustration: visible paper grain, screenprint texture, and restrained halftone in selected shadows. Texture adds character without muddying faces or silhouettes. The image should work as a single composed scene, rather than needing comic panels, speech balloons, or lettering to read as a comic.

The examples combine cream and warm candy pink with saturated turquoise and deep plum. Strong red accents guide the eye. Warm practical lights sit against graphic shadows. This palette is useful for reproducing the examples, but can change for another project; the ink shapes, shading, print texture, and composition define the style.

For action scenes, use a strong diagonal, a clear line of motion through the character's pose, and a few purposeful debris or fabric shapes. For quieter scenes, retain the same ink, colour, and texture treatment with calmer staging. Action, violence, candy gore, mascots, and surreal architecture are subject choices rather than requirements of the style.

## Reusable style prompt

Append this to a description of the scene you want:

```text
Style/medium: bold cel-shaded action-comic illustration. Confident ink contours, solid black and deep plum shadow shapes, crisp graphic lighting, expressive faces, and strong readable silhouettes. Carefully composed environments with selective detail. Tactile paper grain, screenprint texture, and restrained halftone in selected shadows. Warm cream and candy pink with saturated turquoise accents, balanced against deep plum; adapt the palette to the scene while preserving strong value contrast. Warm practical lights against punchy shadows. For action, use dynamic diagonal staging and a clear line of motion; for quiet scenes, use deliberate, readable poses. One polished standalone illustration. Avoid photorealism, smooth 3D rendering, soft watercolor washes, heavy painterly blending, and muddy detail.
```

## Full scene template

Replace the bracketed fields. Remove optional lines that do not apply.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: standalone game concept illustration.

Scene/backdrop: [Location, time of day, and a few defining environmental details.]
Subject: [Character appearance, clothing, expression, and the specific action or interaction.]
Story detail: [One or two visible details that communicate the situation.]
Composition/framing: [Wide environment view, medium character scene, or close portrait; describe the focal point and useful camera angle.]
Lighting/mood: [Light sources and emotional tone.]

Style/medium: bold cel-shaded action-comic illustration. Confident ink contours, solid black and deep plum shadow shapes, crisp graphic lighting, expressive faces, and strong readable silhouettes. Tactile paper grain, screenprint texture, and restrained halftone. Carefully composed environment with selective detail. Warm cream and candy pink, saturated turquoise accents, deep plum shadows; adapt to the requested setting. Dynamic diagonal staging for action, or deliberate poses for quieter moments. Keep the main subject and important environmental details readable.

Constraints: one complete scene. No text, lettering, speech balloons, logos, watermark, UI, panel borders, or montage unless specifically requested. Avoid photorealism, smooth 3D rendering, soft watercolor washes, and muddy painterly blending.

Optional reference image: use the attached image as a reference for ink treatment, shading, palette, and print texture only. Create the new scene described above; do not copy its characters, props, or setting unless explicitly requested.
```

## Original style wording

This is the style portion of the original birthday-scene prompt, preserved for reproducing that particular treatment:

```text
Style/medium: striking cel-shaded action-comic concept painting, bold black and plum ink shapes, sharp graphic shadows, angular dynamic silhouettes, screenprint texture, limited halftone, cream and candy pink with saturated turquoise and crimson jam. Dramatic diagonal action composition with the protagonist caught in a powerful melee swing, candy and confetti describing the motion. Keep environment readable and warm party lights against punchy shadows. Mature dark-comedy action, not a children's illustration. Avoid photorealism and soft painterly rendering.
```

For the closest visual match, attach one of the reference images along with the prompt. Describe explicitly whether you want only its style or also a particular character's appearance preserved. A text prompt establishes a direction; an image reference helps maintain continuity across scenes.
