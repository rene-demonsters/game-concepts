# Art styles

Reusable visual directions and prompt templates for future concept art. These references are independent of any game's final art direction.

- [Comic book / action comic](comic-book.md): bold ink silhouettes, cel shading, warm colour, and textured print finishes.
- [Painted storybook](painted-storybook.md): textured gouache, expressive shapes, soft colour, and warm pools of light.
