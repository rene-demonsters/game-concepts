# Painted storybook

A reusable art direction based on the painted storybook concept art explored for *Make Yourself at Home*. Keeping this reference does not select it as that game's final style.

## The look

Hand-painted gouache illustration with tactile paper grain, opaque layers of colour, and broad, slightly imperfect brushwork. Shapes are simplified and expressive, with edges defined primarily by neighbouring colours and painted shadows. Selective detail in faces, clothing, plants, and furnishings rewards a closer look while keeping the scene readable.

Perspective is gently flattened and deliberately staged. Rooms and objects feel illustrated rather than optically exact, but characters still have clear poses, convincing weight, and enough space around them to communicate an action. Layer foreground, middle ground, and background to build a scene you can explore with your eye.

The examples use soft peach, butter yellow, mint, and dusty lavender, with richer red accents and warm golden pools of light. Shadows introduce cooler colours and depth without overwhelming the softness. This palette reproduces the examples; other projects can change it while retaining the opaque paint, textured surface, expressive shapes, and deliberate staging.

The style can support cozy everyday moments, adventure, comedy, or unsettling events. For action, use a clear gesture and sweeping painted shapes to carry movement. For quiet scenes, let posture, light, and environmental details tell the story. Candy gore, mascots, surreal architecture, and an uncanny mood belong to the original scenes rather than being requirements of the style.

## Reusable style prompt

Append this to a description of the scene you want:

```text
Style/medium: sophisticated hand-painted gouache storybook illustration. Tactile paper grain, opaque layered colour, broad slightly imperfect brushwork, elegant simplified shapes, and expressive faces. Define forms through painted colour and shadow rather than heavy ink outlines. Gently flattened but carefully staged perspective, with clear foreground, middle ground, and background. Selective detail in clothing, furnishings, and scenery; readable silhouettes and poses. Soft peach, butter yellow, mint, and dusty lavender with richer accent colours; adapt the palette to the scene. Warm golden pools of light and cooler painted shadows. For action, use a clear gesture and sweeping shapes; for quiet scenes, use deliberate posture and environmental storytelling. One polished standalone illustration. Avoid photorealism, smooth 3D rendering, comic ink outlines, cel shading, thin transparent watercolor washes, and muddy overblending.
```

## Full scene template

Replace the bracketed fields. Remove optional lines that do not apply.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: standalone game concept illustration.

Scene/backdrop: [Location, time of day, and a few defining environmental details.]
Subject: [Character appearance, clothing, expression, and the specific action or interaction.]
Story detail: [One or two visible details that communicate the situation.]
Composition/framing: [Wide environment view, medium character scene, or close portrait; describe the focal point and useful camera angle.]
Lighting/mood: [Light sources and emotional tone.]

Style/medium: sophisticated hand-painted gouache storybook illustration. Tactile paper grain, opaque layered colour, broad imperfect brushwork, elegant simplified shapes, and expressive faces. Forms defined through painted colour and shadow. Gently flattened but carefully staged perspective, layered depth, and selective environmental detail. Soft peach, butter yellow, mint, and dusty lavender with richer accents; adapt to the requested setting. Warm golden light against cooler painted shadows. Keep the main subject's gesture and important environmental details readable.

Constraints: one complete scene. No text, lettering, logos, watermark, UI, panel borders, or montage unless specifically requested. Avoid photorealism, smooth 3D rendering, heavy comic ink outlines, cel shading, thin transparent watercolor washes, and muddy overblending.

Optional reference image: use the attached image as a reference for gouache texture, painted shapes, palette, lighting, and perspective only. Create the new scene described above; do not copy its characters, props, or setting unless explicitly requested.
```

## Original style wording

This is the style portion of the original birthday-scene prompt, preserved for reproducing that particular treatment:

```text
Style/medium: sophisticated hand-painted gouache storybook illustration, tactile paper grain, broad imperfect brushwork, elegant simplified shapes and expressive faces, flattened but carefully staged perspective. Soft peach, butter yellow, mint and dusty lavender, rich jam accents. Warm golden pools of light, quietly uncanny shadows. Whimsical and mature, cozy yet disquieting. Avoid 3D rendering and comic ink outlines.
```

For the closest visual match, attach one of the reference images along with the prompt. Describe explicitly whether you want only its style or also a particular character's appearance preserved. A text prompt establishes a direction; an image reference helps maintain continuity across scenes.
