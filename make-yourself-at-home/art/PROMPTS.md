# Concept art prompts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. These are alternative visual directions for the same birthday assignment, not final character designs or gameplay screenshots.

## storybook

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: standalone landscape game concept artwork for Make Yourself at Home, a surreal melee action thriller.
Scene: a welcoming pastel indoor birthday play centre becoming an exuberant candy-coated battleground. Elevated three-quarter view, wide landscape composition, readable room geography and characters, polished art direction exploration rather than a screenshot.
Subject: an adult, androgynous returning resident in a comfortable cream cardigan and teal trousers, wielding a ridiculously oversized party-cake serving knife, facing a giant cheerful rabbit entertainer. An old portable cassette recorder sits on a birthday table between them; its trailing tape impossibly loops through an open doorway and emerges through another doorway in the same room. The protagonist is confident and enjoying the confrontation.
Details: warm party lights, cake, soft play structures, balloons, toppled chairs, broken foam animatronic parts, vivid strawberry-jam and frosting splashes, confetti. A cozy domestic breakfast nook is visible beyond a door, impossibly connected to the play centre. A small number of adult party guests watch with affectionate delight. Keep a warm inviting atmosphere alongside spatial unease and dark absurd humor.
Constraints: imaginary original characters; no recognisable existing game characters; no children; no realistic blood, human wounds or anatomical gore, only candy and stuffing; no text, logos, title lettering, UI, watermark, borders or multi-panel montage. Make one complete scene.
Style/medium: sophisticated hand-painted gouache storybook illustration, tactile paper grain, broad imperfect brushwork, elegant simplified shapes and expressive faces, flattened but carefully staged perspective. Soft peach, butter yellow, mint and dusty lavender, rich jam accents. Warm golden pools of light, quietly uncanny shadows. Whimsical and mature, cozy yet disquieting. Avoid 3D rendering and comic ink outlines.
```
