## room-of-your-own-storybook

Asset: `04-room-of-your-own-storybook.png`. Reference: `01-storybook.png`.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: standalone landscape game concept art for Make Yourself at Home, surreal melee action thriller.
Input image role: STYLE AND PROTAGONIST REFERENCE ONLY. Create a completely new scene in the same art direction. Preserve the reference protagonist's adult androgynous appearance, tousled short dark hair, cream strawberry-pattern cardigan, white shirt, high-waisted teal trousers and brown shoes. Do NOT copy birthday setting, cake table, balloons or rabbit.
Mood: cozy welcoming colours, joyful excessive action, dark absurd humour, spatial reality subtly but clearly impossible. Candy gore only: jam, frosting, stuffing; no anatomical or realistic human gore. Original characters, adults only. No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, UI, captions, borders or montage. One complete wide landscape scene, polished environment-and-action concept art.
Scenario: A Room of Your Own.
Setting: a cheerful pastel apartment atrium with three visible levels of warmly furnished rooms, floral wallpaper, balconies, hanging plants, a mint elevator, brass intercom and stair flights that return into the same landing. Different balconies repeat the exact same breakfast table and lamp, a deliberate impossible architectural connection.
Central action: the protagonist enjoys swinging an oversized ornate brass house-key as a melee weapon, smashing a tall smiling concierge automaton made of upholstered cushion blocks; cranberry-jam filling and stuffing erupt in a broad readable arc. A second upholstered concierge approaches with a velvet rope. Beside a comfortable chair at the intercom, a fully clothed adult man in mustard sweater is rising to leave; cloth-covered speaker cables connect his sweater cuffs to the walls like loose puppet strings, no medical restraints or wounds. A friendly resident offers him slippers instead of helping him escape. His hands and the intercom share the same brass bell motif.
Visual narrative: absurd violent eviction/rescue amid genuine domestic comfort, a man who may be the building. Establish all this as one carefully composed coherent location, not panels. Show at least one impossible stair loop clearly, but maintain readable floor and action silhouettes.
Style/medium: Sophisticated hand-painted gouache storybook illustration. Tactile paper grain, imperfect broad brushwork, simplified expressive faces, carefully staged flattened perspective. Peach, butter yellow, mint and dusty lavender, golden light, rich jam accents. Cozy but disquieting. No comic ink outlines or 3D rendering.
```
